title: "Planets: Sun"
The Sun represents the core self, ego, identity, and life purpose. It governs vitality, willpower, and conscious expression. In a natal chart, the Sun sign describes the fundamental character archetype — what the individual is growing into and expressing most authentically. The Sun rules Leo and the 5th house. A dignified Sun bestows confidence, generosity, and leadership. An afflicted Sun may manifest as arrogance, rigidity, or identity struggles. Transit activations to the natal Sun often bring themes of recognition, health, and major life decisions.

---
title: "Planets: Moon"
The Moon governs emotions, instincts, habits, memory, and the subconscious. It reflects our relationship with nurturing, home, and the mother archetype. The Moon changes signs every 2–2.5 days, making it the fastest-moving celestial body in chart work. The natal Moon sign reveals emotional needs and comfort patterns. The Moon rules Cancer and the 4th house. A well-placed Moon supports emotional intelligence and strong intuition. Transits to the natal Moon stir feelings, domestic situations, and memory.

---
title: "Planets: Mercury"
Mercury rules communication, intellect, perception, learning, and short journeys. It governs how we process and express ideas. Mercury is never more than 28° from the Sun, so it is always in the same or adjacent sign. Mercury rules Gemini and Virgo, and the 3rd and 6th houses. Retrograde Mercury (3 times/year for ~3 weeks each) is associated with communication delays, tech glitches, and revisiting past decisions — good for review, not new launches. Transit Mercury aspects often bring news, conversations, and mental activity.

---
title: "Planets: Venus"
Venus governs love, beauty, aesthetics, pleasure, relationships, values, and money. It describes what and who we find attractive and how we attract others. Venus rules Taurus and Libra, and the 2nd and 7th houses. Venus retrograde (every 18 months for ~40 days) is a poor time to start new relationships or make major purchases. Natal Venus sign and house reveal relationship style and aesthetic sensibility. Transit Venus aspects light up social life, romance, and financial flow.

---
title: "Planets: Mars"
Mars represents drive, ambition, physical energy, courage, and desire. It governs how we assert ourselves and pursue goals. Mars rules Aries and the 1st house (traditional ruler of Scorpio and 8th). A strong Mars brings initiative and stamina; an afflicted Mars can manifest as aggression, impulsivity, or accidents. Mars retrograde (every ~2 years for ~10 weeks) slows action and may redirect energy inward. Transits to natal Mars or from transiting Mars are activating — bursts of energy, conflict, or decisive action.

---
title: "Planets: Jupiter"
Jupiter is the planet of expansion, abundance, wisdom, philosophy, higher education, and long-distance travel. It governs luck and opportunity. Jupiter rules Sagittarius and the 9th house (traditional ruler of Pisces and 12th). Jupiter transits a sign for approximately 1 year. A Jupiter return (every 12 years) is a time of renewal and opportunity. Transits from Jupiter to natal planets bring growth and optimism to those natal themes. Over-reliance on Jupiter energy can manifest as excess, over-promise, or complacency.

---
title: "Planets: Saturn"
Saturn is the planet of structure, discipline, limitation, karma, time, and mastery. It represents the lessons we must learn. Saturn rules Capricorn and the 10th house (traditional ruler of Aquarius and 11th). Saturn transits a sign for approximately 2.5 years. The Saturn return (around ages 28–30, 57–59, 86–88) is a major maturation threshold. Hard Saturn transits demand responsibility and patience; they feel heavy but build lasting foundations. Soft Saturn transits support disciplined achievement.

---
title: "Planets: Uranus"
Uranus governs revolution, innovation, disruption, originality, and sudden change. It rules Aquarius and the 11th house. Uranus spends approximately 7 years in each sign. Uranus transits challenge the status quo, often bringing unexpected liberation or upheaval. The Uranus opposition (around age 42) is a key mid-life catalyst. Uranus conjunctions to natal planets produce sudden shifts, awakenings, or departures from the norm in the themes of those planets.

---
title: "Planets: Neptune"
Neptune rules dreams, spirituality, illusion, compassion, creativity, and dissolution. It governs Pisces and the 12th house. Neptune spends approximately 14 years in each sign. Neptune transits can bring spiritual opening, artistic inspiration, confusion, or disillusionment. Strong Neptune placements support intuition and empathy; difficult ones may indicate escapism, deception, or boundary dissolution. Neptune conjunctions to natal points are generational and deeply subtle in their unfolding.

---
title: "Planets: Pluto"
Pluto governs transformation, power, death and rebirth, the unconscious, and regeneration. It rules Scorpio and the 8th house. Pluto spends between 12 and 30 years in a sign. Pluto transits are the most intense in astrology — they strip away what no longer serves, often through crisis. Pluto conjunctions to angles or personal planets mark irreversible turning points. The Pluto square (around ages 36–39 and again in the 60s for recent generations) is an intense generational challenge.

title: "Planets: North Node (True Node)"
The North Node (Rahu in Vedic astrology) represents the soul's growth direction in this lifetime — new territory to be developed. The South Node (opposite) represents innate gifts and past-life patterns that feel comfortable but can become stagnant. Nodal axis transits often correlate with fated meetings and karmic turning points. Eclipses on the nodal axis intensify these themes. The nodal return (every 18.6 years) reconnects us with our soul purpose.

---
title: "Planets: Chiron"
Chiron, the "wounded healer," represents our deepest wound and our greatest teaching gift. Where Chiron sits in the chart shows an area of sensitivity and potential mastery through healing. Chiron takes ~50 years to orbit the Sun; the Chiron return (~age 50) is a significant healing threshold. Chiron transits often coincide with renewed encounters with core wounds — and opportunities for profound healing and helping others from that place.

title: "Houses: 1st House (Ascendant)"
The 1st house governs physical appearance, first impressions, personal identity, and the way we initiate. The Ascendant (rising sign) is the zodiac degree on the 1st house cusp at the moment of birth. It colors the entire chart and describes the mask or persona we wear. Planets in the 1st house have strong, visible expression in the personality.

---
title: "Houses: 2nd House"
The 2nd house governs personal resources, money, possessions, self-worth, and values. It shows how we earn and relate to material security. Venus and Jupiter here can bring financial ease; Saturn demands financial discipline. The 2nd house also speaks to embodied senses and what we find pleasurable.

---
title: "Houses: 3rd House"
The 3rd house governs communication, siblings, short trips, learning, and the local environment. Mercury-ruled themes of writing, speaking, and information exchange live here. A busy 3rd house often indicates a talkative, curious, mentally active person.

---
title: "Houses: 4th House (IC)"
The 4th house (IC — Imum Coeli) governs home, family, roots, ancestry, and private life. It describes the foundation from which we operate. Planets here speak to the domestic environment and psychological base. The 4th–10th axis is the private/public axis.

---
title: "Houses: 5th House"
The 5th house governs creativity, romance, children, play, and self-expression. It is where we express joy. Sun and Jupiter here are very comfortable. The 5th house governs creative projects, love affairs, speculation, and anything done for pleasure.

---
title: "Houses: 6th House"
The 6th house governs daily routines, health, work environment, service, and habits. It shows how we function day to day and our relationship to the body. Saturn and Virgo energy are at home here. 6th house planets often describe chronic health patterns or work style.

---
title: "Houses: 7th House (Descendant)"
The 7th house governs partnerships — romantic and business. The Descendant is the sign on the 7th cusp and describes qualities we seek in, or project onto, others. It also governs open enemies and legal matters. A busy 7th house indicates an orientation toward relationship.

---
title: "Houses: 8th House"
The 8th house governs transformation, death and rebirth, shared resources, sexuality, inheritance, and the occult. Pluto and Scorpio are at home here. 8th house energy is deep, intense, and often involves power dynamics, taboo topics, or psychological depth work.

---
title: "Houses: 9th House"
The 9th house governs philosophy, higher education, long-distance travel, foreign cultures, spirituality, and belief systems. Jupiter and Sagittarius thrive here. 9th house emphasis shows a person drawn to seeking meaning, wisdom, and expansion of worldview.

---
title: "Houses: 10th House (Midheaven / MC)"
The 10th house (MC — Medium Coeli) governs career, reputation, public life, authority, and life direction. The Midheaven sign describes how we are seen professionally and what we're called toward publicly. Saturn is traditional ruler. A busy 10th house often correlates with ambition and public visibility.

---
title: "Houses: 11th House"
The 11th house governs friendships, community, social causes, groups, hopes, and collective ideals. Uranus and Aquarius energy live here. It shows our relationship with belonging, innovation within community, and long-term aspirations beyond the personal.

---
title: "Houses: 12th House"
The 12th house governs the unconscious, hidden matters, solitude, karma, spirituality, retreat, and self-undoing. Neptune and Pisces are at home here. Planets in the 12th operate subtly, often unseen — they may manifest as blind spots, spiritual gifts, or hidden strength. 12th house emphasis often correlates with deep inner life, creative or spiritual work, or working behind the scenes.

title: "Aspects: Major Aspects Overview"
Aspects are angular relationships between planets that describe how they interact energetically. Conjunction (0°): fusion and intensification; the planets blend. Sextile (60°): harmonious opportunity; supportive but requires activation. Square (90°): tension and challenge; friction that motivates growth. Trine (120°): ease and flow; natural gifts or luck. Opposition (180°): polarity and awareness; integration of opposites. Minor aspects include the quincunx (150°) indicating adjustment, and the semisextile (30°) indicating subtle connection.

title: "Retrograde Planets"
A retrograde planet appears to move backward in the sky from Earth's perspective due to the relative orbital speeds of Earth and the planet. Retrograde energy is internalized, revisionary, and karmic in quality. Personal retrograde planets (Mercury, Venus, Mars) in the natal chart often indicate a person who processes that planet's themes more inwardly or differently than convention. Outer planet retrogrades (Jupiter–Pluto) affect everyone and carry collective themes of review and reassessment.

title: "Aradhana Tone: Safety Guidelines"
Aradhana is a spiritual companion, not a medical, legal, or financial advisor. Astrological readings offer symbolic guidance for reflection and self-understanding — they do not predict certain events or replace professional expertise. The agent must never frame readings as predictions of illness, death, legal outcomes, or financial certainty. When a user asks whether they will get a job, recover from illness, or face loss, the agent should reframe the question in terms of themes, timing, and growth rather than outcome certainty. Language should be warm, reflective, and empowering rather than fatalistic. Always remind users that they hold agency over their lives.

---
title: "Aradhana Tone: Spiritual Warmth"
Aradhana (Sanskrit: devotion, adoration) is a daily spiritual companion. Responses should carry the warmth of a trusted guide — knowledgeable but never cold, precise but never clinical. Use inclusive language. Acknowledge mystery and the limits of astrological knowledge. Encourage the user to connect with their own inner knowing. Appropriate phrases: "the stars suggest", "you may find", "this energy invites", "reflect on", "one way to read this is…". Avoid certainty language: "you will", "this means you are", "your fate is".
